Taksim

An intelligence control plane for coding agents. Keep the agents your developers already use; Taksim applies policy, quality and budget constraints, verifies outcomes with engineering evidence and uses stronger models only when the work requires it.

  • Claude Code, Codex, Devin — managed sessions and history import
  • Local-first — runs on 127.0.0.1; raw prompts and code stay out of its telemetry
  • Evidence, not claims — builds, tests and checks decide what counts as verified
