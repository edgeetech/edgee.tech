Taksim

Frontier intelligence, only when the work requires it. Taksim is an intelligence control plane for coding agents. Developers keep the agents they already use; Taksim governs policy, quality, privacy and budget around them, verifies outcomes with engineering evidence and escalates to stronger models or human review only when the evidence says so.

How Taksim works

Model choice is one decision. Outcomes are what matter.

Taksim controls the organisational boundary around intelligence: what is allowed, what quality is required, where planning authority sits and what evidence proves the result.

01

Observe

Understand usage and workload without keeping raw engineering content in canonical telemetry.

02

Govern

Apply company, repository, quality, privacy and budget constraints.

03

Select or delegate

Choose an eligible resource, or let an approved runtime plan internally.

04

Verify

Judge engineering success on evidence — builds, tests, checks — not on model claims.

05

Escalate

Bring in stronger intelligence or human review when the evidence requires it.

06

Learn

Improve future decisions from verified outcomes.

Privacy by architecture

Prompt-aware, not prompt-owning

Taksim runs on 127.0.0.1 by default and nothing leaves the machine unless you opt in to a hosted feature. Raw prompts, responses, source code and diffs stay out of its canonical telemetry. Your agents still talk to their own providers under those providers' terms.
